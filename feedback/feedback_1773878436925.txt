    [PREGUNTA DEL TÉCNICO]
    Como quito e instalo el panel frontal de mi Daikin FTXN?

    [RESPUESTA DEL ASISTENTE]
    ## **📋 Diagnosis**
The front panel of your Daikin FTXN air conditioner needs to be removed or installed.

## **🔧 Solution**
**1.** To remove the front panel, hook your fingers on the tabs located on the left and right sides of the main body. Open the panel until it stops.

**2.** Slide the front panel sideways to disengage the rotating shaft.

**3.** Pull the front panel toward you to remove it.

**4.** To install the front panel, align the tabs of the front panel with the grooves.

**5.** Push the panel all the way in.

**6.** Close the panel slowly.

**7.** Push the center of the lower surface of the panel firmly to engage the tabs.
